# Art sheet for Chapter Two, The Lyre (run in Claude chat with Higgsfield)

Free on the Higgsfield allowance until 13 November. Chapter Two is still a proposal, so this sheet covers every option in `CHAPTER_TWO_PROPOSAL.md`; what is not used costs nothing.

Already collected, do not repeat: the twelve film shots (`tools/src/films/c2`), four backdrops (court, Gibeah, hall, road in `tools/src/art_in/c2`), David's seated lyre clip and the lyre prop.

Only one video and one image run at a time. A video takes 5 to 10 minutes, and there are 24 here, so allow three to four hours. An image part can run in a second chat while a video part runs.

**If time runs short, do them in this order:** Part 1, then Part 2 (lead, lyre_stand, shield_block), Part 3 (Saul's three), Part 4 (donkey's three, wolf_prowl), Part 5, Part 6, then the rest.

Results can stay in the Higgsfield account; they are collected from there. Keep the names so each can be told apart.
