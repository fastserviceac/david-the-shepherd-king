## B. David calls the four named sheep

Names from the tranche 1 session, not yet approved by you. The Portuguese uses the names as the Portuguese Bible spells them (Rachel is Raquel, Devorah is Débora). Short calls come out better recorded together in one take and split afterwards, so the four are recorded as one line per language.

| `call_noga` | David | Noga! Come, Noga. | Noga! Vem, Noga. |  | 33 |

What the names mean, from the Hebrew: *rachel* is the ordinary word for a ewe; *devorah* is a bee; *nogah* is brightness (a son of David has the name, 1 Chronicles 3:7); *shai* is a gift brought as tribute (Psalm 76:11). Please check these before they go in; I have not checked them against a Hebrew lexicon.

## How it will be recorded

Once you say yes to a group, it is handed to a helper that records it, saves the files to `audio/narration/<id>.mp3` and `audio/narration/pt/<id>.mp3`, and reports back only the file paths. As before, `eleven_v4` ignores pause tags, so each line is one call. You listen before anything is committed.
